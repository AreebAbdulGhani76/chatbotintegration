FAQ

Honest answers, no fluff.

If something isn't answered here, just ask us directly on the fit call — we'd much rather tell you "we aren't the right fit" than sell you something that won't help your business.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes, 100%. The site lives on your custom domain and all written copy, photography, and brand assets are entirely yours. If you ever decide to leave, we export your website cleanly with zero ransom tactics.

How long is the contract commitment?

It is strictly month-to-month after the initial launch month. Our ongoing responsibility is to continually earn your business through measurable results and great support, not to lock you into a rigid 12-month contract.

How fast do changes and edits ship?

Most small edits (updating prices, seasonal promos, adding a new technician photo, updating hours) are completed and live within 24 hours on Growth. On our Scale tier, priority requests typically ship the same day.

Can you work with our existing website or domain?

In most cases, yes. During our 15-minute fit call, we'll review your current setup together and give you a straight answer on whether it's best to restructure your current site or launch fresh with a clean foundation.

What if I don't have professional photos or customer reviews yet?

Don't worry. We provide proven templates and simple automated review capture flows that help you gather authentic 5-star Google reviews and technician job-site photos during your first 30 days.

Is Aureon only designed for HVAC businesses?

HVAC is where we started, but our proven local service framework works seamlessly for plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, commercial cleaning, garage doors, pest control, and restoration services.
